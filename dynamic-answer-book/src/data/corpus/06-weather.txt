霧會散。 || The fog will lift.
霧還要留一陣子。 || The fog will stay a while.
等能見度夠了再走。 || Walk when you can see far enough.
看不見也走得，路是直的。 || You can walk without seeing. The road is straight.
風向變了。 || The wind has changed.
逆風，仍前進。 || Headwind. Go on anyway.
順風不要大意。 || Don't get careless in a tailwind.
北風裡不適合告白。 || A north wind is a poor time to confess.
南風來時再出發。 || Leave when the south wind arrives.
雨是一陣子的事。 || The rain is a passing matter.
這場雨會下很久。 || This rain will last.
雷聲比雨大，別被嚇到。 || The thunder is louder than the rain. Don't be startled.
閃電過後再數秒。 || Count the seconds after the lightning.
天會晴。 || The sky will clear.
不要等放晴才快樂。 || Don't wait for clear skies to be glad.
雲很低，人要蹲一下。 || The clouds are low. Crouch a little.
月亮換一輪再問。 || Ask again when the moon turns.
就著這輪月決定。 || Decide by this moon.
新月適合開始。 || The new moon is for beginning.
滿月適合說完。 || The full moon is for finishing what you say.
缺月不是殘缺。 || A partial moon is not a defect.
潮還會來。 || The tide will come again.
退潮時去撿。 || Go pick it up at low tide.
漲潮前記得回來。 || Come back before the tide rises.
岸在後面。 || The shore is behind you.
船該出海。 || The boat should go out.
船先靠岸。 || Bring the boat to shore first.
橋是安全的。 || The bridge is safe.
橋下的水比橋重要。 || The water under the bridge matters more than the bridge.
過橋，不要回頭看水。 || Cross the bridge. Don't look back at the water.
回頭看一眼就好。 || One look back is enough.
不要回頭。 || Don't look back.
路會自己變短。 || The road will shorten itself.
路比你想的長，帶水。 || The road is longer than you think. Bring water.
岔路選有樹的一邊。 || At the fork, take the side with trees.
岔路選有光的一邊。 || At the fork, take the side with light.
兩條路最後會相會。 || The two roads meet later.
兩條路不會再相遇。 || The two roads will not meet again.
腳下這塊石頭要踢開。 || Kick this stone out of the path.
石頭留下，當記號。 || Leave the stone as a mark.
腳印會被雨洗掉。 || The rain will wash the footprints away.
留下腳印沒有關係。 || It is all right to leave footprints.
冬天適合結束。 || Winter is for endings.
冬天先別開始大事。 || Don't start the large thing in winter.
春天再看一次。 || Look once more in spring.
春天會太吵，現在就決定。 || Spring will be noisy. Decide now.
夏天的熱情先打折。 || Discount summer's enthusiasm.
夏天適合動身。 || Summer is for setting out.
秋天適合整理。 || Autumn is for putting things in order.
秋天的告別是乾淨的。 || Autumn's farewell is clean.
霜還未到，果子留樹上。 || The frost has not come. Leave the fruit on the tree.
果子熟了，摘。 || The fruit is ripe. Pick it.
青的先別摘。 || Don't pick it while it is green.
落葉不是失敗。 || Fallen leaves are not failure.
把落葉掃開，路就現了。 || Sweep the leaves aside, and the path appears.
雪蓋住的不一定消失。 || What the snow covers is not necessarily gone.
等雪化。 || Wait for the snow to melt.
雪地上可以走，慢慢。 || You may walk on the snow. Slowly.
冰很薄。 || The ice is thin.
冰承受得住。 || The ice will hold.
日出前的冷是暫時的。 || The cold before sunrise is temporary.
黃昏不適合做決心。 || Dusk is a poor hour for resolve.
黃昏適合原諒。 || Dusk is right for forgiveness.
正午太亮，看不見影子。 || Noon is too bright. You cannot see the shadow.
影子在你前面，跟上。 || Your shadow is ahead of you. Follow it.
影子在你後面，你在領先。 || Your shadow is behind you. You are ahead.
星很少，但是夠。 || There are few stars, and they are enough.
不要等流星。 || Don't wait for a shooting star.
流星看見了，願可以許。 || You saw the meteor. You may make the wish.
願許小一點。 || Make the wish smaller.
河會把你帶到下一座城。 || The river will carry you to the next city.
不要下河，走岸。 || Don't enter the river. Walk the bank.
井水很深，繩子夠長。 || The well is deep. The rope is long enough.
繩子不夠，先別下去。 || The rope is short. Don't go down yet.
山不轉，你轉。 || The mountain will not move. You will.
這座山可以不爬。 || You don't have to climb this mountain.
爬到能看見的地方就好。 || Climb only until you can see.
山頂沒有你要的，半山有。 || The summit doesn't have it. The hillside does.
谷底會有回音，你先喊。 || The valley will echo. Call first.
不要在谷底做決定。 || Don't decide at the bottom of the valley.
地平線還會往後移。 || The horizon will move back again.
你已經夠遠了。 || You are far enough.
再遠一點點。 || A little farther.
回家的路你認得。 || You know the way home.
家的定義要改了。 || The meaning of home must change.
家還是原來那個。 || Home is still the same one.
燈光在前方，不是錯覺。 || The light ahead is not an illusion.
那是別人的窗，不是你的。 || That is someone else's window, not yours.
你的窗在側邊。 || Your window is off to the side.
夜航可以，看著燈。 || You may sail at night. Watch the lights.
靠港。 || Come into port.
錨放下。 || Drop the anchor.
錨拉起來。 || Raise the anchor.
帆收一點。 || Reef the sail a little.
帆可以張滿。 || You may set the sail full.
舵交給你。 || The helm is yours.
這一段讓別人掌舵。 || Let someone else take the helm for this stretch.
浪會過。 || The wave will pass.
大浪前先蹲下。 || Crouch before the large wave.
岸邊的人在招手。 || Someone on the shore is waving.
那不是招手，是告別。 || That is not a greeting. It is a farewell.
告別揮短一點。 || Make the farewell wave short.
種子先埋好。 || Bury the seed properly.
還不是播種的日子。 || This is not the day to sow.
土壤太濕。 || The soil is too wet.
土壤剛好。 || The soil is right.
除草。 || Weed it.
有些草留著。 || Leave some of the weeds.
果實分給人。 || Share the fruit.
果實先留給自己一點。 || Keep a little of the fruit for yourself.
路燈壞了，仍走得。 || The streetlamp is out. You can still walk.
換一條有燈的路。 || Take a road that has lamps.
十字路口停三秒。 || Stop three seconds at the crossing.
綠燈了，走。 || The light is green. Walk.
黃燈，停。 || The light is amber. Stop.
紅燈不是針對你。 || The red light is not personal.
抄近路會迷路。 || The shortcut will lose you.
近路這次是真的。 || This shortcut is real.
遠路比較好走。 || The long way walks better.
腳起泡了就停。 || If your foot blisters, stop.
再走十分鐘就到。 || Ten more minutes, and you are there.
你已經到了，只是不信。 || You have arrived. You just don't believe it.
目的地會換一張臉。 || The destination will wear a different face.
認得出來的，就是它。 || If you recognize it, it is the one.
不認得就不是。 || If you don't recognize it, it isn't.
風景會變，方向不用變。 || The view will change. The direction need not.
方向錯了，趁早轉。 || The direction is wrong. Turn while you can.
方向是對的，步伐太急。 || The direction is right. The pace is too rushed.
