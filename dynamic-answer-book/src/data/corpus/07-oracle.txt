這次不必排隊。 || You don't have to wait in line this time.

這已經是奇蹟，只是很安靜。 || This is already a miracle. It is quiet.

你還沒選擇，只是在搖。 || You have not chosen. You are only swaying.

不要繞。 || Don't go around.
